## Purpose
`POL-AI-001` establishes enterprise governance principles, operational boundaries, and accountability mandates for all artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies deployed within the Enterprise HR AI platform. As machine learning algorithms, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines, and cross-encoders increasingly support human capital operations, formal governance is essential to prevent algorithmic bias, mitigate disparate impact, eliminate autonomous decision-making in adverse employment actions, and guarantee absolute transparency in AI-derived guidance.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all algorithmic components and intelligence pipelines in the Enterprise HR AI system, including:
1. The supervised attrition classification model (`logreg_balanced_threshold_0.40`).
2. The SHAP-based feature importance and explainability engine (`shap.LinearExplainer`).
3. The hybrid retrieval engine (combining `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` dense vectors with `BM25Okapi` sparse indices).
4. The cross-encoder reranking stage (`cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`).
5. The grounded generative response model (`google/flan-t5-base`).
6. All downstream API endpoints (`/predict`, `/recommendations`, `/rag/ask`).

---

## Definitions
- **Decision-Support System:** An analytical platform that synthesizes complex organizational data to provide recommendations, risk signals, and contextual intelligence to human practitioners without executing autonomous operational actions.
- **Autonomous Employment Action:** Any algorithmic execution of an HR action—including termination, compensation adjustment, demotion, hiring, or performance grading—without direct human intervention.
- **Grounding Verification:** The architectural constraint enforcing that generative language models generate factual answers derived strictly from audited enterprise context retrieved from the trusted knowledge base.
- **Parametric Hallucination:** The generation of unverified, plausible-sounding factual assertions by an LLM originating from pretraining memory rather than enterprise source documents.

---

## Policy Rules

### Rule 1: Strict Decision-Support Mandate
The Enterprise HR AI platform is classified strictly as a Decision-Support System. Autonomous employment actions executed by software algorithms are strictly prohibited across all business units. No algorithmic score, probability, risk tier, or LLM-generated response may directly execute, trigger, or finalize:
- Involuntary termination or contract separation.
- Disciplinary actions, formal warnings, or performance improvement plans.
- Compensation adjustments, salary bands, bonus allocations, or equity grants.
- Mandatory reassignment or unilateral job transfer.

### Rule 2: Transparency and Provenance Guarantee
Every insight surfaced by the platform must provide full lineage and explainability:
- Attrition risk predictions must present the top individual SHAP feature drivers (e.g., `OverTime`, `YearsSinceLastPromotion`) explaining why the score was generated.
- RAG assistant responses must surface audited provenance metadata, including source file name, section, document title, and retrieval similarity score.
- Unaudited or black-box predictions that cannot provide feature-level attribution are non-compliant and prohibited from operational dashboards.

### Rule 3: Preserving Human Agency and Override
HR business partners, People Analytics analysts, and departmental managers maintain complete authority to override, reject, or adjust any recommendation generated by the platform. Exercising an override shall not require executive approval and shall carry no punitive consequences for the reviewer. Discrepancies between human judgment and model outputs must be captured to inform quarterly model evaluation per `POL-MONITOR-001`.

### Rule 4: Grounded Knowledge Retrieval Enforcement
Generative AI capabilities (specifically the `/rag/ask` service) must enforce prompt-level grounding. If verified context retrieved by the hybrid search pipeline does not contain the information required to answer a user's prompt, the system must explicitly emit the standardized refusal response:
*"I do not have enough verified enterprise information to answer that question."*
The model is strictly prohibited from extrapolating external knowledge, inventing benefits, or hallucinating organizational commitments.

## Procedure
1. **Model Ingestion & Registration:** Any machine learning model or retrieval index must be registered with a completed Model Card (`docs/model_card.md`) detailing intended use, training data provenance, evaluation metrics, and limitations before deployment.
2. **Runtime Inference:** During prediction or retrieval execution, models consume validated inputs adhering to the schema (`app/validation/employee_schema.py`).
3. **Audit Trail Logging:** All API requests, input parameters, generated scores, retrieved source chunks, and execution timestamps are logged to `logs/app.log`.
4. **Human Review Gate:** Platform outputs presented in `frontend/dashboard.py` display prominent visual indicators stating: *"AI-Generated Decision-Support — Requires Professional Human Review."*

---

## Exceptions and Limitations
- **Synthetic Data Baseline:** The current model suite is trained exclusively on the IBM HR Analytics synthetic dataset (1,470 records). Performance characteristics (ROC-AUC 0.8060, Recall 0.7872, Precision 0.3426) reflect controlled synthetic scenarios and must not be extrapolated to real organizational workforces without recalibration.
- **Correlation vs. Causality:** Feature attributions (such as SHAP values for `BusinessTravel_Travel_Frequently`) represent statistical correlations within historical data, not verified causal mechanisms.

---

## Human Review Requirements
In accordance with `POL-REVIEW-001`:
1. All retention risk reviews initiated from platform flags must involve at least one certified HR professional.
2. Managers must conduct personal 1-on-1 career discussions rather than referencing model scores directly with employees.
3. Overrides must be logged in the People Analytics case management system for governance reporting.
