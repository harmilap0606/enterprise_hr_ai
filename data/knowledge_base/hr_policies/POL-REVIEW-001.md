## Purpose
`POL-REVIEW-001` defines the mandatory operational protocols, ethical safeguards, and review workflows governing human oversight across all AI-assisted insights generated within the Enterprise HR AI system. Algorithmic intelligence provides powerful statistical pattern detection across large workforce datasets, but models cannot capture human nuance, qualitative workplace context, personal emergencies, or organizational restructuring realities. By establishing an uncompromised "human-in-the-loop" requirement, this policy ensures that human judgment, empathy, and professional ethics remain the ultimate authority in all talent decisions.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all business leaders, HR business partners, People Analytics analysts, and people managers interacting with:
- Predictive flight-risk flags generated by `POL-MODEL-001`.
- Workforce retention tiering and stay-interview workflows under `POL-RISK-001`.
- Skill gap deficiency classifications generated under `POL-SKILL-001`.
- Automated upskilling course recommendations generated under `POL-LEARN-001`.
- Occupational mobility pathways modeled under `POL-CAREER-001`.
- Knowledge answers generated by the generative RAG assistant (`/rag/ask`).

---

## Definitions
- **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):** A governance architecture requiring that every algorithmic prediction, score, or recommendation must be reviewed and validated by an authorized human professional before any action is executed.
- **Human Override:** The formal rejection, modification, or cancellation of an algorithmic recommendation by an HR professional or manager based on qualitative context or domain expertise.
- **Qualitative Context:** Workplace information not captured in digital schemas, such as recent organizational realignments, bereavement, interpersonal dynamics, or undocumented projects.
- **Confirmation Bias Mitigation:** The active analytical practice of challenging high-risk or deficiency flags rather than presuming algorithmic infallibility.

---

## Policy Rules

### Rule 1: Mandatory Human Oversight Gate
No employment action, developmental intervention, or retention discussion may proceed solely on the basis of an algorithmic output. Every risk flag ($\ge 0.40$), skill gap diagnosis, or course recommendation must pass through a documented Human Oversight Gate:
- The human reviewer must evaluate whether the model output aligns with known qualitative realities.
- The reviewer must independently verify whether the input data attributes (e.g., tenure, promotion history, overtime) accurately reflect the employee's current situation.

### Rule 2: Absolute Right of Human Override
HR business partners and managers have the full, uncompromised authority to override any algorithmic output generated by the platform:
- If an employee is flagged as `HIGH` risk (e.g., score 0.85), but the manager knows the employee recently accepted an exciting new project lead role and is thriving, the reviewer may mark the risk flag as "Overridden — Stable".
- If the skill gap engine flags an employee as deficient in `Google Docs` or `Speaking`, but the manager has observed exemplary performance in these areas, the reviewer may adjust the profile.
- Exercising an override shall require only a concise rationale and shall never incur negative performance repercussions for the reviewer.

### Rule 3: Protection Against Direct Algorithmic Confrontation
Managers and HR professionals are strictly prohibited from confronting employees directly with algorithmic scores, labels, or probabilities:
- A manager may never state to an employee: *"The AI model flagged you as an attrition flight risk"* or *"The algorithm determined that you have a high skill gap."*
- Discussions must be framed entirely around constructive professional inquiry: *"I wanted to check in on your current workload, your recent project experience, and how we can support your career growth."*

### Rule 4: Mandatory Review for Vulnerable or High-Impact Cohorts
Enhanced human review is required in scenarios where model uncertainty is structurally higher:
- **Low Match Confidence Roles:** Roles with `match_confidence: low` or `very_low` in `jobrole_onet_mapping.csv` (`Healthcare Representative`, `Sales Executive`, `Manufacturing Director`, `Research Director`, `Manager`).
- **Missing Survey Data:** Employees lacking engagement records (the 50.3% survey null cohort).
- **High Severity Gaps:** The 71 employees flagged with `HIGH Severity` skill gaps.

## Procedure
1. **Insight Ingestion:** The reviewer accesses the "HR/Manager View" on `frontend/dashboard.py` and filters for employees in their operational area.
2. **Contextual Evaluation:** The reviewer examines the four core data panes: Model Risk & SHAP Drivers, Survey Metrics (if non-null), Role Architecture, and Skill Gap Recommendations.
3. **Qualitative Verification:** The reviewer cross-references current operational context (recent team changes, project milestones, personal leaves).
4. **Determination:**
   - **Validate:** The reviewer agrees that developmental or retention support is warranted and initiates action.
   - **Override:** The reviewer documents that the algorithmic output does not reflect operational reality, recording the override reason.
5. **Audit Recording:** All review outcomes and override reasons are logged in the People Analytics registry to support quarterly model audits under `POL-MONITOR-001`.

---

## Exceptions and Limitations
- **Synthetic Demonstration Framework:** The review procedures specified here govern the simulated enterprise workflows of this project. They do not supersede statutory employment contracts or legal counsel in real corporate entities.
- **Time Constraints:** High-risk cohorts must be reviewed within 30 days of model inference to ensure interventions remain timely and relevant.

---

## Human Review Requirements
All provisions of this policy constitute human review requirements. Specifically:
1. Every review must be signed off by a qualified HR professional.
2. Override rates exceeding 40% in any department trigger a mandatory model audit under `POL-MONITOR-001`.
